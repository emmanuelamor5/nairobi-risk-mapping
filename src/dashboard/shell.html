GraphSAGE config

Structural deprivation head
Architecture

Two SAGEConv layers, mean aggregation, hidden width 64, dropout 0.5, then a linear layer to three deprivation classes.

Training

Adam, learning rate 0.005, weight decay 0.0005. Stratified 60/20/20 split. Sprint 3 target is validation macro-F1 of at least 0.60. The three-settlement target is 0.70. Inputs are footprint area, distance to water, sanitation, and road, and a roof-material score.

Colab results

Kibera Sprint 3 validation macro-F1 was 0.9382 (accuracy 0.9371). On the three-settlement run the pooled validation macro-F1 was 0.8813: Kibera 0.8972, Mathare 0.8755, Mukuru 0.7453. That cleared the 0.70 target.

Attention U-Net

Flood hazard head
Architecture

U-Net encoder and decoder with an attention gate on each skip connection. Default width starts at 32 channels. The default stack has 9 inputs: six Sentinel-2 bands plus elevation, slope, and topographic wetness.

Training

BCE plus Dice loss, Adam, optional dropout and weight decay, cosine learning-rate schedule, and early stopping. Transfer target is IoU of at least 0.60. The final flood F1 target is 0.75.

Colab results

The first training run reached a train IoU of about 0.77 while validation stayed near the flood-everywhere baseline of about 0.31. After the event-based label, the three-settlement Colab run reached a pooled IoU of 0.7201 and F1 of 0.8373, above the 0.60 and 0.75 targets.

Fusion engine

Late-fusion policy matrix
Rule

The flood probability and the deprivation class are read off a fixed matrix. Compensated resettlement is only reached when hazard is high and high deprivation is a majority, not a mere plurality.

Colab evaluation

The Sprint 5 Colab run fused 18,005 parcels. The justice audit passed, with 1,265 resettlements. River-corridor lift was 0.929, below the bar of 1, so these actions are not a validated flood-hazard map.
